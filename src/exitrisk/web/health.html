What is wrong, and where?

Pick the accounts having trouble. The check reads every region in use: alarms, AWS Health, recent changes, refused calls, load balancers, containers, functions, databases, queues, networks, certificates, DNS, VPC flow logs, error spikes in the busiest log groups (Logs Insights) and quotas, then ranks the ten most likely causes. It also runs the Outage Risk checks for other issues in the accounts, and lists data sources to turn on. Read only; Logs Insights is billed by AWS at US$0.005 a GB read.
